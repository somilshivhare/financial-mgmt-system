**Visual:** App flow: login → dashboard → invoices → collection plan → reports. Clean UI, no code.

**Script:**

*"You log in and see your business at a glance—how much you’ve invoiced, how much you’ve collected, what’s overdue, and where the bottlenecks are. Your customers, your purchase orders, your invoices, and your payments are all connected. No more hunting across files and folders."*

*"You plan collections by person, by unit, by month. You know exactly who’s following up on what—and when. When payments come in, you record them in one click. Your dashboard and reports update automatically. Aging, outstanding, GST, reconciliation—all in one place, ready for management and audit."*

*"And when you need muscle on the ground—liaison with utilities, PSUs, or government bodies—NB Aurum’s team steps in. They know the processes, the people, and the paperwork. You get regular updates and MIS. You stay in control; they handle the heavy lifting."*
